01About

I'm a systems administrator from Belgrade with about three years at RTS (Radio Television of Serbia), where I work on email administration, virtualization and security systems built on the Fortinet stack. I started out in networking, moved through virtualization, and the part I enjoy most is the security side: understanding what an alert actually means and how to make systems harder to break.

Outside work I run a segmented homelab with a production zone, an isolated attack lab, a FortiGate firewall and a Wazuh SIEM. I use it to practise web and network vulnerability assessment and to learn how real infrastructure behaves under pressure.

Next, I want to move into cloud and DevSecOps engineering: building infrastructure as code, CI/CD pipelines and network automation with security designed in from the start, not added later.
